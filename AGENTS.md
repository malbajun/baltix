# Contribution rules

## Gherkin tables

Use three spaces per indentation level. Align every `|` separator vertically in
each table, including both edges and inner columns. Keep separator positions on
the three-column indentation grid: each distance between adjacent separators
must be a multiple of three. Left-align values with one space after `|`; round
content widths up to a multiple of three and pad before the next separator.
Preserve meaningful spacing within values, including expected RPM spec lines.

Apply this rule to all tables in feature files you add or edit, including step
argument tables and Examples tables.
